# Bronze and Bolt

A 0 A.D. (0.28.0) content mod for Greeks and Romans.

## Changes from the stock game

- Only Athens, Macedon, Sparta and Rome are selectable.
- Champion units have ~1.2× regular-soldier stats (HP, armour, damage), with proportionally reduced cost.
- Siege engines are heavily nerfed (HP, armour, damage), with reduced cost.
- Structures have 80% less HP and armour.
- Structures build 2.5× faster.
- Structures cost 80% less.
- Defense towers can be built twice as close together.
- All players start in Town Phase automatically.
- City Phase requires a barracks, stable, market and defense tower (not auto-researched).
- Walls require City Phase.
- Stables require a forge (blacksmith).
- Buildings are not capturable by default; a "Capturable Buildings" match-setup option re-enables capture.
- Buildings at 50% HP or below catch fire and smoke, scaling with how low the HP is.
- Burning buildings lose 1 HP per second until repaired or destroyed.
- Market trade upgrades are auto-researched at game start.
- The temple's unit health-regeneration upgrade is auto-researched at game start.
- Ranged units fire glowing cyan energy projectiles.
- Healers (priests) float instead of walking.
